The idea in one line

Olm can only hit the side it's looking at. When the special is due, nobody is on that side, so Olm loses it.

What counts as a basic: anything fired from Olm's head: magic, ranged, coloured spheres, burn, fire wall, acid. Not basics: Crystal Burst, Lightning and Swap (and the healing hand later).

The mage skip, step by step

1

Roles

You are on the mage side (left) on long range, hitting the mage hand. A thrall helps, because splashes break the skip. Your melee guy stays on the melee side (right) and also attacks the mage hand from range. He must never step past his marked tile, or the head looks at the centre.

2

Why the head comes to you

Olm's head has three positions: left, middle and right. It only turns when Olm acts (every 4 ticks), it turns toward the side with the most players, and if nobody is where it's looking, it turns and loses that action. On the null, Olm doesn't attack. Its head swings toward the claw that took the most damage in the last 4 ticks. You're both hitting the mage hand, so the head comes to your side every cycle. That's why Basic 2 is yours.

3

The loop: MIDDLE → RIGHT → LEFT

You only need 3 tiles at the head: the tile left of the head, the centre, and the tile right of the head (the pack's two head tiles plus the centre). Tank Basic 2 on the centre tile. Then step one tile right, out of the mage-side view. When the special is due, Olm sees nobody on your side, turns its head right, and loses the special. As soon as the head turns, run back LEFT.

4

Setting it (start of a phase or after it breaks)

Tank 2 basics. On the 2nd, the head must be turned fully to your side. Then step right, and go back left when the head turns. The skip is set. Shortcut: if a special lands while the head faces the melee side, run left and it's set. If a special lands while the head faces your side, tank one, step right, and it's set.

5

Timing tips

Click as soon as you see the head start to move; the turn has already happened. Your click takes effect on the next tick, so click one tick early. Tank Basic 2 on the middle tile so a fire wall can't trap you.

#1 mistake

Running right as soon as the head turns to you. You must tank Basic 2 in the middle first. If you leave early, Olm loses a basic instead and the special still comes.

It broke

You start seeing specials again (Crystal Burst, Swap, Lightning). Don't panic: look at which side the special happened on, then set it again (step 4).

Melee guy breaks it

Hitting the melee hand harder than you hit the mage hand, or stepping past his tile, makes the head go the wrong way on the null.

Head stuck in the middle

If players bunch up near the centre, Olm faces the middle. For your Basic 2 the head must be turned fully to your side. If it looks at the middle, tank that attack and go back to your side to keep it set.

Phase powers (they count as basics)

Each standard phase Olm rises with one power. In the 2nd-to-last phase it uses all three. A phase attack replaces about 1 in 3 of Olm's normal attacks (1 in 2 with all powers), so keep counting it as a basic.

Acid

Acid spray: green pools on the floor. Don't stand in them (3–6 damage a tick).

Acid drip: the target leaves a pool on every tile they walk off. Don't drip over your route tiles or onto your teammate.

Flame

"Burn with me!": the target burns for 5 every few ticks, and it spreads to anyone right next to them ("I will burn with you"). Keep a gap.

Fire wall: two walls trap the target. Walk out the open end, or put it out with water. The centre tile under the head can't be fire-walled.

Crystal

Falling crystals: the target gets a red ring and crystals drop where they stand. Keep walking.

Crystal bombs: they explode after a few seconds. Be 5+ tiles away (15 damage at 4 tiles, more the closer you are).
